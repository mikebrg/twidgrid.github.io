  • ReStory is a chill, narrative-driven shop management simulator from the creators of I Am Future, set on the bustling streets of mid-2000s Tokyo and featuring an in-depth device repair system. In this game, you run an electronics repair shop, meticulously restore nostalgic Y2K-era devices, including officially licensed Atari consoles, and interact with customers to experience a branching storyline with multiple endings that depend on your choices.
