  • Play as a woman who's an axe-wielding serial killer in this light-hearted puzzle game about decapitating men. In New Detroit, authorities are hunting a serial killer they call Mr. Freezy after hundreds of severed heads are discovered hidden inside freezers across the city. Behind the murders is a mysterious woman kidnapping men and locking them away in her basement workshop. You don’t play as the victims. You help prepare them. Move, swap, and line up heads perfectly so they can all be taken off in a single slice. Every move matters as you try to collect the most heads possible in the fewest number of turns. But the deeper you go, the stranger things become.
