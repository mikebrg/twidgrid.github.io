Worthy fighters from all over the land have come to Dagsion, the capital city of the Dagdan Empire, to compete in the Heroic Games for the chance to win one wish. Among the combatants are four heroes tied to the fate of the world. Whose fortune will lead them to victory? Explore the city of Dagsion. Between matches, you can visit numerous locations within the city to prepare. Recruit allies around town, level up Blessings at temples, hone your skills at the training ground, build bonds of friendship at inns, and more. Travel across the World Map. Venture out into the surrounding land to complete quests, gather resources, and defeat roaming enemies for experience.
