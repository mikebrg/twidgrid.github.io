  • Resident Evil 4: Gold Edition is the definitive bundle of Capcom’s Resident Evil 4 Remake. Originally released in February 2024 for PC, PS4, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S, the bundle has expanded to newer platforms, including the Nintendo Switch 2. It compiles the critically acclaimed base game alongside all major paid post-launch content.

    The Gold Edition includes a massive chunk of content that expanding upon Leon S. Kennedy's grueling rescue mission in rural Europe:

    • The Base Game: The fully reimagined, modernized version of the 2005 survival horror classic.

    • Separate Ways DLC: A complete story campaign following Ada Wong. It fills in unanswered questions from the main story and lets you experience the events from her unique perspective.

    • Extra DLC Pack: A collection of digital cosmetics, weapons, and useful upgrades containing:

    • Costumes & Filters: "Casual" and "Romantic" outfits for both Leon and Ashley, alongside "Hero" and "Villain" filter options for Leon.

    • Accessories: "Sunglasses (Sporty)" for Leon.

    • Deluxe Weapons: The Sentinel Nine pistol and Skull Shaker shotgun.

    • Soundtrack Swap: The ability to swap to the Original Ver. soundtrack.

    • Treasure Map Expansion: Reveals additional hidden treasures in the game world that aren't available on standard in-game maps.
