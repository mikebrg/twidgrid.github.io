  • SpeedRunners 2: King of Speed is a fast-paced competitive platformer and a sequel to one of the most beloved indie games of the 2010s. Pick a character of your choice from a vibrant cast of eccentric superheroes, both returning and new, and face off against your rivals in frenetic running races for up to 8 players per lobby, locally or online. The original multiplayer gameplay formula of SpeedRunners is timeless, unofficially dubbed as the ‘Mario Kart of 2D platformers’. With the sequel, our goal is to carefully bring this unique ‘SpeedRunners feel’ into the new era without compromising on the depth and fluidity of the movement system while still keeping it easy to grasp. On top of that, King of Speed introduces vibrant high-definition graphics and improved netcode.
