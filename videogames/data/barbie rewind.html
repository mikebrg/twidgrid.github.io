  • Decorate the DreamHouse through the decades, while playing classic Barbie games in this cozy and creative game. Barbie Rewind pulls you back in time through iconic eras of Barbie with stunning pixel art and retro style. Your bestie, Barbie, invites you into her world and entrusts you to update her DreamHouse with your unique style, using items from 65+ years of Barbie history. Choose from three retro DreamHouse designs for your exterior facade, walls, and floors. You can even mix-and-match them from room to room. Style each room with a fabulous collection of furniture, decor, and accessories, inspired by real Barbie playsets throughout the decades. From a classic pink vanity to a ‘90s inflatable chair, make the DreamHouse unique to your style and creativity. Barbie has also left you her game collection of 16 Barbie video games from 1991 through 2007. Titles include Barbie Pet Rescue, Barbie Horse Adventures: Blue Ribbon Race, and the never-before-released game, Barbie: Vacation Adventure. Play through the collection to complete additional quests and unlock more items.
