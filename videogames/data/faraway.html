  • You are a shooting star that refuses its fate of disappearing in the ether, to instead go travel space and draw constellations through its journey. Faraway is a one-button game, with a simple set of rules finely balanced to adapt to different playing levels, within an always renewed universe that is procedurally generated in real time - based on score -, resulting in an infinitely replayable game. Like Steph Thirion's first game Eliss, Faraway is set in a bizarre and lonely outer space, was 100% created - design, coding, music and sound - by one person, and all its graphics are drawn by code, resulting in a rarely personal sensory experience.
