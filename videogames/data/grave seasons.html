  • Can't decide who to date, what school to go to, or what team to pick as a winner? This application will help you to decide. You simply create a group or groups and add members or items to each of the groups. Random generator then eliminates members or items one by one and then generates a winner. The generator/eliminator can be run repeatedly for the same group and the results are statistically evaluated. The results can be reset if desired.
