  • The Golden Age of Piracy is coming to an end - as the high seas are commandeered by the Crown’s pirate hunters, an era of adventure and plunder comes to a close... unless you can save it. Corsair Cove is a city builder that challenges you to think tall as you build without constraints on cliff-faces and hilltops and manage complex production chains that span across great lengths and heights. Manufacture everything from spyglasses to cannons, equip your crew, and build mighty ships to take to the seas and wage war against the Crown. Raid and pillage nearby islands to acquire what you can’t make for yourself, exploring uncharted lands to uncover great treasures and threats alike. Above all, keep your crew in high spirits as your numbers grow - it’ll take thousands of pirates to keep the Jolly Roger flying!
