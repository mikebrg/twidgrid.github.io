  • The saga of Granblue Fantasy: Relink unfolds on the Nintendo Switch™ 2. Known for its dynamic combat, ensemble of versatile characters, and thrilling online co-op, this latest version of Relink features expanded solo and multiplayer content—bringing endless adventure to skyfarers both new and old. Gameplay Features Band together for exhilarating battles. Form a party of four and depart for battle in this epic action RPG. Each character sports distinct combat mechanics, allowing for a whole crewful of unique playstyles. Weave these playstyles together using satisfying party-based attacks—like link attacks and chain bursts—to really make combat soar. Even recruits fresh to action gaming can take advantage of Assist Mode to reach the story's heart-pounding conclusion. Embark on an endless variety of co-op quests. A wide range of quests become available as the adventure unfolds. From unique boss fights to horde-survival missions, all quests are playable offline or online with up to four players. Whether it's casual questing or ultra-challenging end content, there's something for everyone to enjoy.
