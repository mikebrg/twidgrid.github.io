  • Fallen Tear: The Ascension is a 2D Metroidvania set in a seemingly boundless world where you will fight dangerous beasts, defy corrupt gods, and face a multitude of challenges all in an attempt to bring balance back to your world. Embark on this journey with Hira, a mysterious young boy who seeks the truth about his forgotten past. Along the way, bond with a multitude of fully-voiced characters to unlock new skills and abilities that will aid you in your battle through environments that constantly change and react to your choices - all in classic, hand-drawn animation. This game is inspired by Elden Ring’s level design, Zelda: Breath of the Wild’s open world design, Suikoden and Valkyrie Profile’s JRPG goodness, and Shadow of the Colossus’ secrets building.
