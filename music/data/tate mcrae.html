  • Tate Rosner McRae (born July 1, 2003) is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and dancer based in the United States. She first gained prominence as a contestant on the American reality television series So You Think You Can Dance in 2016. She signed with RCA Records and gained early recognition for her extended plays (EPs) All the Things I Never Said (2020) and Too Young to Be Sad (2021); the latter became the most streamed female EP of 2021 on Spotify and was preceded by the single "You Broke Me First", her first US Billboard Hot 100 entry.

    In 2022, McRae released her debut studio album, I Used to Think I Could Fly, which peaked at number three on the Canadian music charts and was supported by the single "She's All I Wanna Be". Her second studio album, Think Later (2023), explored a more pop-oriented sound and produced the multi-platinum single "Greedy", which became one of the bestselling songs of 2024 globally. In 2025, McRae released her third studio album, So Close to What, which topped the US Billboard 200 and yielded the global top-ten hits "Sports Car", "Revolving Door", and "Tit for Tat". That same year, she scored her first US number-one single as a featured artist on Morgan Wallen's "What I Want" and earned her first Grammy Award nomination for the soundtrack single "Just Keep Watching".
