  • Douglas "Dougie F" Ford is an American rapper, producer, and highly successful songwriter from Orange, New Jersey. Initially breaking out as a recording artist with his unique pop-trap and Jersey-club-infused flow, he has grown into a major powerhouse behind the scenes, writing chart-topping hits for some of the biggest global stars.

    He has earned two Grammy Awards for his extensive contributions to the music industry. He is a credited songwriter on massive global tracks including Drake's "Jimmy Cooks", SZA's "Open Arms", Jack Harlow's "First Class", Travis Scott's "Escape Plan", and Tyla's "Chanel". Beyond songwriting, his own music features heavy hitters. He joined Cardi B on "Man Of Your Word" and Rosalía on "Porcelana". He was officially awarded the Key to the City of Orange, NJ, and August 2nd was declared Douglas "Dougie F" Ford Day.
