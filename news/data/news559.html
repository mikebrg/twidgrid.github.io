Music streaming platform Tidal announces ban on fully AI-generated music

2026, November 27

Home Latest News Search

  • Music streaming platform Tidal announces ban on fully AI-generated music

    2026, November 27

    United States / United Kingdom

    Music streaming service Tidal announced on November 27 that it would prohibit fully AI-generated music from its platform. The decision comes amid growing concerns within the music industry about the rapid development of artificial intelligence tools capable of producing complete songs, including vocals, lyrics and instrumental arrangements. The technology has made it increasingly difficult to distinguish between recordings created by human musicians and those generated entirely by computer systems.

    The decision reflects a wider debate about copyright ownership, artistic originality and the financial interests of professional musicians. Artists and record labels have expressed concern that AI-generated recordings could flood streaming platforms, potentially reducing royalty payments and making it harder for genuine musicians to reach audiences. The issue has become particularly controversial as AI systems are increasingly trained using existing musical recordings, sometimes without the explicit permission of their creators. Tidal's move could encourage other streaming platforms to introduce stricter rules governing AI-generated content and provide greater transparency about how music is produced.
