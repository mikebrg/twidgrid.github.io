  • Five major US leagues compete for fans on one extraordinary night

    2026, October 26

    United States

    Monday marked a rare sports equinox, when all five major North American professional leagues—the MLB, NFL, NBA, WNBA and NHL—were in action on the same night. The schedule included Game 3 of baseball's World Series between the Tampa Bay Rays and Milwaukee Brewers, alongside NBA and NHL regular-season games and Game 5 of the WNBA Finals between the Golden State Valkyries and Atlanta Dream.

    The WNBA Finals were tied at two games apiece following Golden State's 67–62 victory in Game 4 on 24 October. The league announced on 26 October that Game 6, if required, would be played in Atlanta on Thursday, 29 October, with its start time moved to accommodate scheduling and broadcasting arrangements.

    Why it matters: The unusual overlap showcased the competition for audiences across major sports and highlighted the growing commercial profile of women's professional basketball.
