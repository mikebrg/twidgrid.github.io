  • Australian music legend Molly Meldrum dies aged 83

    2026, November 23

    Australia

    Australian television presenter, music journalist and producer Ian "Molly" Meldrum died on November 23 at the age of 83. He became one of Australia's most influential entertainment personalities through his work on the ABC television music programme Countdown, which he helped transform into an essential platform for discovering new music. His famous expression, "Do yourself a favour", became widely recognised among Australian music fans and musicians.

    Meldrum played an important role in promoting both Australian and international performers, helping introduce audiences to artists including AC/DC, INXS and Kylie Minogue. He also interviewed major international stars, including Elton John and members of the British royal family. His contribution to the entertainment industry was recognised through numerous honours, including induction into the ARIA Hall of Fame in 2014. His death prompted tributes from musicians, broadcasters and political leaders, who remembered him as a major figure in the development of Australia's modern music industry.
