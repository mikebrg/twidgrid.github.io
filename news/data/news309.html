Saudi Arabia Investigates Airport Defences After Deadly Houthi Attacks

  • Saudi Arabia Investigates Airport Defences After Deadly Houthi Attacks

    2026, October 12

    Saudi Arabia / Yemen / Iran

    Saudi Arabia is investigating how its air-defence systems failed to prevent a devastating missile and drone attack on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport. The weekend attacks killed at least 13 people and injured more than 300, according to reports. A further attack on Sunday killed one person and injured 11, prompting authorities to extend the airport closure. Airlines have suspended services, while aviation authorities in the United States and Europe have issued safety warnings.

    The Iran-backed Houthi movement in Yemen has claimed responsibility for the attacks. Saudi Arabia is considering its military response, while Iran has offered to mediate. The crisis threatens to widen the regional conflict and further disrupt shipping through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, an important route for international trade and energy supplies.

    Why it matters: The attacks expose serious vulnerabilities in Gulf security and threaten international aviation, energy markets and maritime trade.
