EU Imposes Nearly 1,600 New Sanctions Targeting Russia's War Industry

2026, October 12

Home Latest News Search

  • EU Imposes Nearly 1,600 New Sanctions Targeting Russia's War Industry

    2026, October 12

    European Union / Russia / Ukraine

    European Union foreign ministers have approved nearly 1,600 new sanctions listings targeting Russia's military-industrial complex. EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said more than half relate to the supply chains used to manufacture ballistic missiles. The measures also target individuals and organisations accused of supporting Russia's war effort, marking the largest expansion of the bloc's sanctions listings since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.

    The announcement comes as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy accuses Russia of violating a proposed energy ceasefire. Russian strikes have continued to hit Ukrainian infrastructure, including energy facilities, despite US President Donald Trump's announcement that the two sides had agreed to stop attacking energy systems. European leaders have expressed scepticism about whether the arrangement will hold.

    Why it matters: The new sanctions aim to make it harder for Russia to manufacture weapons, while the uncertain ceasefire highlights the continuing difficulties in achieving a negotiated end to the war.
