Amazon commits more than €16 billion to German AI and cloud infrastructure

2026, October 20

Home Latest News Search

  • Amazon commits more than €16 billion to German AI and cloud infrastructure

    2026, October 20

    Germany

    Amazon plans to invest more than €16 billion in Germany during 2027, expanding its cloud computing, artificial-intelligence, satellite internet and media operations. The announcement includes €4.8 billion for additional infrastructure in Amazon Web Services' Frankfurt cloud region, a second robotics and AI research laboratory in Berlin, and logistics projects expected to create more than 3,000 jobs.

    The investment comes as Germany seeks to strengthen its industrial competitiveness and attract capital into advanced technologies. Chancellor Friedrich Merz has also highlighted plans to quadruple the country's AI computing capacity by 2030. Amazon's expansion reflects growing demand for computing infrastructure as businesses adopt AI services and cloud-based applications.

    Why it matters: The investment could create jobs, strengthen Germany's digital infrastructure and intensify competition among global cloud providers. It also highlights the scale of investment required to support increasingly sophisticated AI systems.
