  • Middle East tensions intensify as Yemen’s Houthis report new Saudi airstrikes

    2026, October 13

    Saudi Arabia / Yemen / Middle East

    Authorities linked to Yemen’s Houthi movement accused Saudi Arabia of launching overnight airstrikes against areas under Houthi control. The claims followed air-raid sirens and reported explosions in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia’s capital. Saudi Arabia had not immediately commented on the alleged strikes, so the claims should be treated as unconfirmed.

    The escalation comes amid wider regional instability and growing concerns over shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. The situation threatens energy supplies, commercial aviation and diplomatic efforts to prevent the conflict from spreading further. Air India has announced cancellations of flights to and from Riyadh, Dammam and Jeddah until 17 October.

    Source: Associated Press — Middle East developments
