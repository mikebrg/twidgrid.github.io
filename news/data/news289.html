2026, October 3

Home Latest News Search

  • Brazil Heads to a Pivotal Presidential Vote in a Tight Race

    2026, October 3

    Brazil

    Brazil goes to the polls on Sunday in a closely watched presidential election pitting incumbent Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva against Flávio Bolsonaro, the son of former president Jair Bolsonaro. Polls indicate Lula is likely to lead the first round but that neither candidate is expected to secure the majority needed to avoid a runoff on 25 October.

    The election has implications well beyond Brazil because Latin America's largest economy is a major commodity producer and an important diplomatic power. Lula has resisted closer alignment with U.S. President Donald Trump's regional agenda, while Flávio Bolsonaro has positioned himself much closer to Trump. The contest is therefore also being viewed as a test of the balance between left- and right-wing politics across Latin America.
