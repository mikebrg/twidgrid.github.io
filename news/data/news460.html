Poland Declares National Mourning After Deadly Kraków Shooting

2026, November 8

  • Poland Declares National Mourning After Deadly Kraków Shooting

    2026, November 8

    Poland

    Poland began two days of national mourning following a mass shooting in Kraków that left six people dead and 15 others injured. The 24-year-old suspect, who was also wounded by police, allegedly held far-right extremist views. The attack took place in the city's historic Main Square, a major tourist destination. Polish authorities described the incident as a possible act of terrorism and strengthened security across the country.

    Investigators also discovered the bodies of the suspect's parents and sister at their family home. The incident has intensified concerns about extremist radicalisation and public safety in Poland, particularly following a series of violent incidents involving young people in recent months.
