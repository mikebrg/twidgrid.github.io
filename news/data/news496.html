Japan's economy unexpectedly contracts as household spending weakens

  • Japan's economy unexpectedly contracts as household spending weakens

    2026, November 15

    Japan

    Japan's economy unexpectedly contracted during the July–September quarter of 2026, raising concerns about the country's economic recovery. The preliminary figures showed that gross domestic product declined at an annualised rate of 0.1%, reversing the expansion recorded in the previous quarter. Weaker household consumption and pressure from inflation contributed to the disappointing performance, highlighting the difficulties facing Japanese consumers.

    The figures create a challenge for the Bank of Japan as it considers the timing of further interest-rate increases. Policymakers must balance the need to control inflation against the risk of placing additional pressure on an economy that is already struggling to maintain growth. The announcement also affected currency markets, with investors reassessing the outlook for the Japanese yen and the possibility of further monetary-policy changes.
