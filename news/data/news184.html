Dolly Parton, queen of country music, dies at 80

2026, August 25

Home Latest News Search

  • Dolly Parton, queen of country music, dies at 80

    2026, August 25

    United States

    Yes — this is a major loss for the music world. Dolly Parton died on August 25, 2026, aged 80, in Nashville, with her family announcing her death.

    Dolly Parton was far more than a country singer. Her career stretched across music, film, television, business and philanthropy, making her one of America's most recognisable cultural figures.

    Some of her best-known songs include:

    “Jolene”

    “9 to 5”

    “I Will Always Love You” — famously later turned into a huge international hit by Whitney Houston “Coat of Many Colors”

    “Here You Come Again”

    Reuters reports that she sold more than 100 million records and received 11 Grammy Awards, including a Lifetime Achievement Award. She was also inducted into both the Country Music Hall of Fame and the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.

    Her philanthropy may be her greatest legacy

    One of the most remarkable parts of Parton's life was her charitable work. Her Imagination Library provided free books to children, while she also contributed to medical research, including a $1 million donation that supported COVID-19 vaccine research.

    She also built Dollywood into a major attraction and became an important economic and cultural figure in her home state of Tennessee.

    An extraordinary songwriter

    Parton wrote around 3,000 songs during her career. Perhaps fittingly, one of the most enduring lines associated with her own philosophy was that her songs were her legacy.

    And there's an almost poetic connection with the news of her death: her family shared her final message, “I will always love you.”

    That sentence, of course, carries extraordinary weight given that I Will Always Love You became one of the most famous songs in popular music history.

    Dolly Parton wasn't simply the “Queen of Country Music.” She became an institution — and very few entertainers manage to remain genuinely beloved across generations in the way she did.
