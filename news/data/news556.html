Global stock markets rebound as investors return to technology shares

2026, November 27

  • Global stock markets rebound as investors return to technology shares

    2026, November 27

    United States / China / Europe

    Global financial markets staged a strong recovery on November 27 following several days of heavy losses, particularly among technology and artificial intelligence companies. Investors returned to battered technology stocks, taking advantage of lower share prices after concerns about excessive AI valuations had triggered a significant market correction. Japan's Nikkei gained 2.1%, while Asian shares excluding Japan rose 1.8%. European markets also advanced, with the STOXX 600 gaining approximately 1%.

    Wall Street followed the positive trend when trading resumed after the Thanksgiving holiday. The Dow Jones Industrial Average finished 1.18% higher, the S&P 500 gained 1.60%, and the Nasdaq Composite advanced 2.31%. Semiconductor companies performed particularly well, with Micron rising 7.6% and Marvell gaining 7.3%. China's CSI 300 also surged 4.2% following government commitments to provide additional support to financial markets. Despite the recovery, investors remained cautious about the sustainability of AI-related spending, corporate valuations and the possibility of further US interest rate increases. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq were still heading towards their worst monthly performances since 2008.
