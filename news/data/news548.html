China launches Shenzhou-24 with Pakistan's first astronaut heading to its space station

  • China launches Shenzhou-24 with Pakistan's first astronaut heading to its space station

    2026, November 25

    China / Pakistan

    China launched its Shenzhou-24 crewed spacecraft on November 25 from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre, marking an important development in its international space programme. The mission attracted particular attention because it included a Pakistani astronaut, who was set to become the first foreign national to visit China's Tiangong space station. The mission represents a significant step in China's efforts to expand international cooperation in space exploration.

    The mission also includes an ambitious scientific programme involving joint experiments between Chinese and Pakistani researchers. Four collaborative scientific experiments are planned aboard the Chinese space station, covering areas of research and technological development. China has increasingly positioned Tiangong as an alternative international research platform alongside the International Space Station, particularly as its space programme continues to expand. The participation of a Pakistani astronaut demonstrates Beijing's intention to strengthen scientific and technological partnerships with other countries while increasing its influence in international space exploration.
