  • Riyadh Airport Attack Escalates Middle East Conflict

    2026, October 11

    Saudi Arabia / Yemen / United States

    Saudi Arabia has extended the closure of its main international airport in Riyadh following a missile attack that killed 12 people and injured more than 300. Authorities have also moved schools in the capital to online learning. The Saudi-led coalition has blamed Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi movement for the attack, while the Houthis have renewed warnings against using Saudi airports and airspace.

    The escalation threatens to draw the United States more directly into the conflict. President Donald Trump has said he is considering whether to support Saudi military strikes against the Houthis. A broader confrontation could threaten shipping through the strategically important Bab el-Mandeb Strait, compounding existing disruption to global energy supplies.

    Why it matters: Further attacks could disrupt international aviation, threaten commercial shipping and deepen the wider Middle East war.

    Sources: Associated Press — Riyadh airport closure and casualties · Reuters — Houthis renew warnings over Saudi airports
