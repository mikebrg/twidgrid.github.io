Marc Márquez makes history with eighth MotoGP world championship

2026, November 23

Home Latest News Search

  • Marc Márquez makes history with eighth MotoGP world championship

    2026, November 23

    Portugal / Spain

    Spanish motorcycle racer Marc Márquez secured the 2026 MotoGP World Championship following the Portuguese Grand Prix, achieving his eighth premier-class world title. The Ducati rider finished eighth in the race, which was sufficient to secure the championship with one round remaining. His achievement was particularly remarkable because he had overcome a 102-point deficit earlier in the season, producing one of the sport's most impressive championship comebacks.

    The Portuguese Grand Prix itself was won by Marco Bezzecchi, who finished ahead of Jorge Martín, with Raúl Fernández completing the podium. Márquez's eighth premier-class crown places him alongside legendary Italian rider Giacomo Agostini in the record books for premier-class titles. The championship represents another extraordinary achievement in Márquez's career, particularly after the injuries and difficult seasons that had threatened to end his dominance in motorcycle racing.
