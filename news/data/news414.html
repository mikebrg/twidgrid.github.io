  • Channel 4 Partners With Disney+ to Expand Streaming Access in Britain and Ireland

    2026, October 29

    United Kingdom / Ireland / United States

    British broadcaster Channel 4 has signed a deal with Disney+ to make many of its programmes available on the streaming platform in Britain and Ireland from early 2027. Under the partnership, selected productions, including the drama Maya, starring Bella Ramsey, are expected to appear on Disney+ at the same time as on Channel 4's own platforms. The agreement represents a significant collaboration between a British public-service broadcaster and an international streaming company.

    The partnership could broaden the international audience for British television while giving Disney+ subscribers access to additional locally produced programming. It also reflects a changing entertainment industry in which traditional broadcasters increasingly collaborate with global streaming services to reach viewers and compete for attention. For Channel 4, the arrangement offers another route to distribute its original productions, while Disney+ strengthens its catalogue of British and Irish content.
