  • CrowdStrike announces $43.9 billion acquisition of cybersecurity rival Zscaler

    2026, November 23

    United States

    Cybersecurity company CrowdStrike announced an agreement to acquire Zscaler in an all-stock transaction valued at approximately $43.9 billion. The acquisition brings together two major players in the cybersecurity industry at a time when businesses are facing increasingly sophisticated cyberattacks and growing security risks associated with artificial intelligence.

    Under the agreement, Zscaler shareholders will receive 1.135 CrowdStrike shares for every Zscaler share they own, representing a premium of approximately 34% over Zscaler's previous closing price. Existing CrowdStrike shareholders will own approximately 84.1% of the combined business. The companies already share around 2,000 customers through an existing partnership. The merger is expected to create a major cybersecurity provider offering both computer and mobile device protection and cloud-based network security. Completion is anticipated during the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028.
