  • Oil Prices Plunge 7% as Markets Welcome Hopes of Middle East Peace

    2026, November 2

    United States / International

    International oil prices fell approximately 7% following renewed optimism that diplomatic negotiations between Iran and the United States could ease disruptions to energy supplies. Brent crude dropped to around $105 per barrel, while the US dollar weakened against several major currencies. Investors also turned their attention towards the approaching US midterm elections and several important central-bank decisions.

    Global stock markets remained unsettled as investors reassessed expensive artificial-intelligence investments. The S&P 500 finished down 0.5%, while the Nasdaq lost 1.1%. Concerns about corporate borrowing, technology valuations and the profitability of AI infrastructure continued to influence investment decisions.
