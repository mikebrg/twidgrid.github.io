  • Milwaukee Brewers Move Within One Victory of Historic World Series Title

    2026, October 27

    United States

    The Milwaukee Brewers defeated the Tampa Bay Rays 10–1 in Game 3 of the World Series on 26 October, taking a commanding 3–0 lead in the best-of-seven championship. Jackson Chourio collected four hits, while rookie pitcher Robert Gasser delivered an outstanding performance, allowing just one hit over six innings. Milwaukee scored eight runs in the fifth inning, setting a World Series record for runs scored with two outs in a single inning.

    The Brewers are now one victory away from securing the first World Series championship in franchise history. Their dominant performance has placed Tampa Bay under enormous pressure, as only one team in major-league postseason history had previously recovered from a 3–0 deficit in a best-of-seven series. Game 4 is scheduled for 27 October, giving Milwaukee an opportunity to complete a historic championship run.
