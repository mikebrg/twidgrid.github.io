North Korea Fires Ballistic Missiles After South Korean Hypersonic Test

2026, October 12

  • North Korea Fires Ballistic Missiles After South Korean Hypersonic Test

    2026, October 12

    North Korea / South Korea / Japan

    North Korea has launched multiple suspected ballistic missiles towards the sea off its eastern coast, according to South Korean and Japanese authorities. South Korea's military reported that one missile travelled more than 900 kilometres before falling into the sea. The launches follow South Korea's recent test of a hypersonic weapon and represent another escalation in the two countries' increasingly tense military relationship.

    South Korean officials described the launches as a demonstration of force in response to Seoul's weapons test. Japan has also expressed concern, while regional security tensions remain elevated amid stalled diplomatic efforts over North Korea's nuclear and missile programmes. Pyongyang has continued developing weapons designed to challenge regional missile-defence systems.

    Why it matters: Further missile development increases the security risks for South Korea and Japan and complicates efforts to revive nuclear diplomacy on the Korean Peninsula.
