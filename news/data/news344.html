Street Fighter tops the box office as an Elizabeth Holmes documentary surprises Hollywood

2026, October 19

  • Street Fighter tops the box office as an Elizabeth Holmes documentary surprises Hollywood

    2026, October 19

    United States

    The latest North American box-office results put Street Fighter, a new adaptation of the popular video-game franchise, in first place with approximately $31 million in domestic ticket sales and $51 million worldwide. The film's opening was accompanied by an unexpected success: You Can See Everything, a documentary examining Elizabeth Holmes and her final days before imprisonment, earned around $15 million despite playing in only 855 cinemas.

    The documentary's performance was particularly notable given its nearly three-hour runtime and limited release. Its strong attendance suggests that audiences remain interested in high-profile corporate scandals and true stories, even in a market dominated by established franchises. The weekend's overall ticket sales were also substantially higher than the equivalent period last year.

    Why it matters: The results demonstrate the continuing commercial strength of video-game adaptations while showing that documentaries can attract significant audiences when a subject captures public interest.
