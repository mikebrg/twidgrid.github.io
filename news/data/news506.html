  • Technology shares lead another decline on Wall Street

    2026, November 17

    United States / China / Taiwan / South Korea

    US stock markets fell on Tuesday as investors continued reducing their exposure to technology companies, particularly those involved in artificial intelligence. The S&P 500 declined 1% to 7,340.53, the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1.6% to 50,759.42, and the Nasdaq Composite lost 0.7%. Nvidia and other volatile AI-related stocks were among the main contributors to the decline, while financial shares also weighed on the market.

    The weakness extended into international markets, with technology-heavy exchanges in Taiwan and South Korea also experiencing losses. Investors were concerned about restrictions imposed by Washington on China's access to advanced American AI chips, as well as uncertainty surrounding Nvidia's leadership and its forthcoming financial results. The sell-off reflects growing questions about whether the enormous investment in AI infrastructure will generate sufficient returns to justify current company valuations.
