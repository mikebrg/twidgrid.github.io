  • Lee Alexander McQueen (17 March 1969 – 11 February 2010) was a British fashion designer and couturier. Known for his unconventional designs, he was one of the most influential fashion designers in the world from the early 1990s until his death by suicide in 2010. His brand, Alexander McQueen, is now owned by Gucci and remains active. McQueen was presented with four British Designer of the Year awards as well as being named the International Designer of the Year in 2003. That same year, he was also made a Commander of the Order of the British Empire for his work in fashion.
