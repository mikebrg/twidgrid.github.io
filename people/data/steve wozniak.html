  • Stephen Gary Wozniak (born August 11, 1950), also known by his nickname Woz, is an American technology entrepreneur, electrical engineer, computer programmer, and inventor. Wozniak co-founded Apple Computer Company (now Apple Inc.) in 1976 with his early business partner Steve Jobs. Through his work at Apple in the 1970s and 1980s, he became widely recognized as one of the most prominent pioneers of the personal computer revolution.

    In 1975, Wozniak started developing the Apple I: 150  into the computer that launched Apple when he and Jobs first began marketing it the following year. He was the primary designer of the Apple II, introduced in 1977, known as one of the first highly successful mass-produced microcomputers, while Jobs oversaw the development of its foam-molded plastic case and early Apple employee Rod Holt developed its switching power supply.
