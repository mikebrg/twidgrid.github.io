  • Heartland Retail (a branch of payment processing giant Heartland Payment Systems and parent company Global Payments) is a cloud-based retail management and point-of-sale platform specifically optimized for multi-location independent retailers, apparel boutiques, and franchise brands.

    Unlike generalized competitors like Square, Heartland Retail separates its architecture from food service to target deep retail pain points. It places its engineering focus on unmatched data reporting, complex inventory grids, and back-office multi-store tracking.
