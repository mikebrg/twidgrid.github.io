  • Acumatica Manufacturing Edition is a cloud-native Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) suite built for small to mid-sized discrete, batch process, and mixed-mode manufacturers. Running on a single database alongside Acumatica's core financials, distribution, and CRM, it connects product engineering, shop-floor execution, inventory, and accounting in real time—with a signature consumption-based pricing model that includes unlimited users.
