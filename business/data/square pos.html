  • Square is an all-in-one cloud-based point-of-sale (POS) and payment processing platform owned by Block, Inc.. Originally famous for its iconic little white headphone-jack card readers, it has grown into the US POS market leader, serving over 4 million sellers globally. The platform is highly favored by small-to-medium businesses because it offers a full ecosystem combining software, built-in payment processing, and proprietary hardware.
