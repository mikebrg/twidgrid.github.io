  • Sage 100 (formerly MAS 90 and MAS 200) is a modular, mid-market Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and accounting software suite designed for small to medium-sized businesses in manufacturing, wholesale distribution, and professional services. Known for its strong financial core, extensive customization options, and large third-party add-on ecosystem, it helps growing companies bridge the gap between entry-level accounting tools (like QuickBooks) and complex enterprise ERPs.
