  • KORONA POS (developed by Combase, based in North Las Vegas) is a high-speed, cloud-based point-of-sale and inventory management software engineered for high-volume, niche retail and ticketing operations. Unlike competitors like Square, which dominate general storefronts, KORONA carved out its market share by focusing heavily on highly regulated retail verticals—such as liquor stores, vape/smoke shops, convenience stores, cannabis dispensaries, thrift stores, and hybrid high-traffic attractions.

    Its core engineering philosophy centers on two things: processing agnosticism (letting you choose your own payment merchant) and lightning-fast checkout speeds designed to completely eliminate customer line congestion.
