  • Rain POS (developed by Rain Retail Software based in Provo, Utah) is a cloud-based all-in-one point of sale, e-commerce, and automated marketing platform engineered specifically for specialty retail niches. While systems like Square are designed for general retail or food service, Rain POS differentiates itself by building out-of-the-box workflows for very narrow, operational-heavy retail markets. It is highly popular among craft & quilt shops, music instrument stores, dive & ski shops, and outdoor sports outfitters.
