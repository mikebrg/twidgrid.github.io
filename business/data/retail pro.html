  • Retail Pro is an enterprise-grade, globally localized retail management and point-of-sale (POS) platform built explicitly for specialty retailers, multinational brands, and complex franchise chains. Headquartered in Folsom, California, Retail Pro operates across more than 130 countries, positioning itself far away from self-serve platforms like Square. Instead, it competes directly with enterprise structures like Erply and NetSuite by acting as a highly configurable, database-heavy foundation for global brick-and-mortar commerce.
