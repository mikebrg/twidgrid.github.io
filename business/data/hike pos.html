  • Hike POS is an Australian-based, cloud-native omnichannel retail point of sale and inventory management platform. Launched in 2014, it is engineered primarily for small-to-midmarket independent retailers.

    While systems like Square emphasize a rigid ecosystem, Hike positions itself as an open-framework retail hub. It specializes in seamlessly bridging physical brick-and-mortar stores with major e-commerce platforms—allowing real-time, central management of products, orders, and customers across channels.
