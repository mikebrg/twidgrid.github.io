  • HealthCare Software (HCS) is a prominent Australian digital health company co-founded in 2005 by John Elkerton, a hospital pharmacist who now serves as the firm's Chief Clinical Information Officer.

    Headquartered in Hobart, Tasmania, the company specializes in building native SaaS and cloud-based software solutions. Its core platforms are deployed extensively in public and private hospitals, emergency services, and community care settings across Australia, the UK, and Canada.
