  • Bindo POS (developed by Bindo Labs) is a cloud-based, iOS-driven omnichannel point of sale and business management solution built for retail, food & beverage, and hospitality operations. Founded in 2010 in New York, the platform has scaled heavily into an international provider. It serves over 10,000 global points of sale across 12 countries, with a massive presence in major Asian commerce hubs like Hong Kong, Singapore, and Malaysia.

    Bindo sets itself apart by operating as a multi-store local commerce network, aiming to effortlessly bridge physical storefront layouts with digital e-commerce marketplaces and hyper-localized delivery logistics.
