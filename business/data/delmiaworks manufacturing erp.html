  • DELMIAWorks Manufacturing ERP (formerly IQMS) is an end-to-end, single-database Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Manufacturing Execution System (MES) designed specifically for mid-market discrete, repetitive, and batch manufacturers. Owned by Dassault Systèmes, it connects shop-floor machine data directly to top-floor financials, inventory, quality management, and SOLIDWORKS CAD engineering workflows without relying on third-party add-ons.
