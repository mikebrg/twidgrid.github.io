    Services: Electronic Health Records (EHR): Stores and manages patients' medical histories, diagnoses, medications, test results and treatment plans.

    Hospital Management: Supports inpatient care, emergency departments, admissions, discharge and patient flow.

    Outpatient and Primary Care: Manages appointments, consultations, clinical records and preventive healthcare.

    Specialist Medical Software: Provides solutions for oncology, cardiology, radiology, surgery, laboratory testing and pharmacy management.

    Patient Portals: MyChart allows patients to access health records, schedule appointments, communicate with healthcare providers and manage aspects of their care.

    Billing and Revenue Cycle Management: Handles patient registration, medical billing, insurance claims and payments.

    Data Analytics and Reporting: Helps healthcare organisations analyse clinical outcomes, operational performance and population health.

    Healthcare Interoperability: Enables authorised sharing of medical information between participating healthcare organisations.

    Telehealth and Patient Engagement: Supports virtual consultations, remote patient monitoring and digital communication.

    Artificial Intelligence (AI): Offers AI-assisted clinical documentation, decision support and administrative workflow automation.

  • Epic Systems Corporation is an American privately held healthcare software company based in Verona, Wisconsin. Founded in 1979, it develops large-scale software systems for electronic health records. It is the largest electronic health records vendor by market share. According to the company, more than 325 million patients have an electronic record in Epic.
