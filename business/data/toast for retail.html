  • Toast POS is an industry giant famously built from the ground up for food service. However, the company offers Toast Retail, an official expansion designed to provide a unified platform for hybrid hospitality-retail businesses.

    If your business is a pure clothing boutique or hardware store, Toast is not the right fit—you should look at Lightspeed or Square Retail instead. But if you run a brewery selling merchandise, a cafe selling coffee beans, or a bakery selling branded apparel, Toast Retail bridges the gap by letting you manage kitchen operations and barcode-heavy merchandise on a single terminal network.
