  • POS Nation is a Charlotte, North Carolina-based turnkey, all-in-one retail POS provider built specifically for independent brick-and-mortar specialty retailers. Founded in 2001, POS Nation differentiates itself by avoiding generic, download-and-go cloud apps. Instead, they focus on providing an entirely pre-configured, plug-and-play setup where the hardware, software, and backend payment processing arrive pre-packaged and customized out of the box.

    The platform serves over 10,000 businesses globally and is particularly dominant in highly regulated, high-velocity retail sectors that demand specialized compliance tools.
