  • Goodbudget (originally launched as EEBA — Easy Envelope Budget Aid) is a digital personal and household budgeting platform based on the traditional cash envelope system. Designed for individuals, couples, and families, it focuses on proactive spending allocation and real-time multi-device budget sharing across iOS, Android, and the web.
